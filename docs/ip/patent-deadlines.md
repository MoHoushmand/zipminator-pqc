# Patent Priority Deadlines: PCT / Paris Convention

Three Norwegian patent applications are on file with Patentstyret. Under the Paris Convention
(Art. 4), the priority period to extend each filing internationally (via a PCT application or
direct national/regional filings) is **12 months from the filing date**. Miss the date and the
priority claim is lost; later disclosures and third-party filings can then be cited against you.

Add the reminder dates below to your calendar / `/schedule`. The "T-30" reminder is the action
trigger: by then counsel must be engaged and the PCT route decided, because PCT preparation and
official-fee payment take weeks.
