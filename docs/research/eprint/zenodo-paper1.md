**Description:**

> We present QRNG-OTP-Destroy, an anonymization scheme achieving information-theoretic irreversibility by binding the mapping key to a physically destroyed quantum random state. We give three cryptographic contributions: (1) an IND-ANON indistinguishability game and a theorem proving zero-advantage security for computationally unbounded adversaries under per-cell tokenization, with a total-variation bound for per-value tokenization; (2) a composition theorem showing clean composition with epsilon-differential privacy, where the DP guarantee survives post-processing by the tokenization step; (3) an ideal anonymization functionality F_ANON in Canetti's simulation paradigm with a realization theorem under the Born rule. Evaluation on the UCI Adult dataset (32,561 records) shows L10 tokenization processes 22,146 unique values in 1.3 seconds while retaining 1:1 structural mapping, in contrast to k-anonymity (22,146 to 111 values) and epsilon-DP (22,146 to 195,470 values).
